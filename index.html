FAQ

Quick answers.

Who can join Dynotic Collective?

Dynotic is open to independent artists. You must own or hold the rights to the music you release. Create an account to get started, and reach out to support if you need help.

Is distribution free?

Yes. Music distribution through Dynotic is free. Original releases earn 80% royalties for the artist.

What can I do in the artist portal?

Set up your profile, create and submit releases for distribution, manage your catalog, and follow the royalty reports and revenue information available for your releases.

What is Cover to Master?

A way to release covers or remixes of songs from the Dynotic Licensed Song list. Royalties are split 60% artist, 30% rights holder, 10% Dynotic.

How long until my release goes live?

Original releases usually go live within 7-14 days, depending on each platform. Cover to Master submissions have no official estimate. Some are approved quickly, others take longer.

Where can I see my royalties?

Royalty reports and revenue information appear in your artist dashboard once received from the platforms. There is no fixed reporting schedule. For questions about a specific report or payment, reach out to support.

How do I get help?

Email support@dynoticcollective.com or message us on Instagram at @dynoticcollective for distribution questions, release issues, and technical matters.
